# Security policy

Anuva stores candidate personal data, so we treat security reports as a priority.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

- Email **security@anuva.io** with a description, steps to reproduce and the affected version or URL.
- Or use GitHub private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability").

We aim to acknowledge reports within 2 business days and to share a fix timeline within 7 days. We will credit reporters who want to be credited once a fix ships.

## Scope

- Anuva Cloud (`*.anuva.io`)
- Code in repositories under `github.com/anuvahq`

Out of scope: denial of service, social engineering, and findings that need a compromised device or account.

## Safe harbour

We will not take legal action against good-faith research that respects user privacy, avoids data destruction and service disruption, and gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.
